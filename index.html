Active Directory Integrations

Problem
Admins connect Active Directory to JumpCloud and choose which system is the source of truth for each user. Setup took too many steps across servers and domain controllers, and configuration errors followed.
What I did
Three years in three rounds: the first connection, delegated authority, and cloud setup with attribute mapping. I moved the feature through two design systems and now share branches with engineering instead of handing off.
