About

I'm a user experience and digital product designer with experience across strategy, design, and development of web-based products with a mobile-first approach. I work with design thinking and agile teams, and currently lead a design and development team.

I'm passionate about finding the intersection between the best possible user experience and business goals — running workshops with stakeholders and users to identify needs and define product direction. I'm a certified Scrum Master and believe agile frameworks are key to building solutions that create real impact.
