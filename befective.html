About

Befective is a SaaS application that helps companies manage time and resources, automatically collecting data on time spent across user activities.

My Contribution

As UX/UI Designer and UI Developer, I optimized user flows, developed customer journeys, and led design sprints end-to-end to validate new directions for the product.

  • Optimized user flows and developed customer journeys; defined the product roadmap
  • Ran A/B testing and information architecture (card sorting)
  • Produced user stories, personas, flows, and high/low-fidelity wireframes
  • Led moderated and unmoderated interviews and usability tests
  • Managed and led design sprints end-to-end

Process

Design SprintReviewed user stories and ran design sprints to define the ideal solution.
IdeaciónPrototypes and mockups built to validate the solutions devised.
ValidationFunctionality built out to measure real results.

Results & Impact

Design sprints surfaced valuable insights that shaped the direction of upcoming releases, producing enough evidence to bring the product meaningfully closer to real user needs.

  • Design sprints surfaced valuable insights for upcoming releases
  • Gathered enough evidence to bring the product meaningfully closer to real user needs

Business Goals

Regular design sprints gave the team a repeatable way to test and validate ideas quickly, keeping the roadmap grounded in evidence rather than assumption.
