Befective

A week of design sprints. Real evidence, not guesses. A roadmap the team actually shipped against.

RoleUX/UI Designer, UI Developer
DurationNot specified
MethodologiesScrum, Design Sprint, OKRs, Kanban

About

Befective helps companies manage time and resources. It automatically collects data on time spent across user activities — the product is only as good as its ability to turn that raw data into something people actually act on.

My Contribution

UX/UI Designer and UI Developer. Sprints were the engine — fast cycles, fast decisions, fast validation.

  • Optimized user flows and developed customer journeys; defined the product roadmap
  • Ran A/B testing and information architecture (card sorting)
  • Produced user stories, personas, flows, and high/low-fidelity wireframes
  • Led moderated and unmoderated interviews and usability tests
  • Managed and led design sprints end to end

Speed as a Design Constraint

A five-day sprint doesn't leave room for a perfect process. It forces prioritization: pick the riskiest assumption, test it, move. That constraint didn't weaken the work — it sharpened it. Every round had to earn its place.

Process

Design SprintReviewed user stories and ran design sprints to define the ideal solution.
IdeaciónPrototypes and mockups built to validate the solutions devised.
ValidationFunctionality built out to measure real results.

Results & Impact

The sprints did their job. Insights came fast, direction became clear, and the evidence was strong enough to actually change what got built next — not just confirm what was already planned.

  • Design sprints surfaced valuable insights for upcoming releases
  • Gathered enough evidence to bring the product meaningfully closer to real user needs

Business Goals

Regular design sprints gave the team a repeatable way to test ideas fast. The roadmap stayed grounded in evidence, not assumption — every cycle, not just the first one.
