Mapeo de Decisiones

A platform for capturing and processing information to document decision-mapping by Public Prosecutor's Offices and Attorneys General across Mexico.

About

Mapeo de Decisiones is a platform built to capture and process information documenting the decision-mapping work of Public Prosecutor's Offices and Attorneys General across Mexico.

My Contribution

As Product Designer, I defined project scope, documented data and process flows, and led information architecture, prototyping, and testing work from beta through full release.

  • Defined project scope; documented data and process flows
  • Ran information architecture (card sorting), user stories, personas, and flows
  • Produced high and low-fidelity wireframes and interactive prototypes
  • Ran usability and heuristic testing; led QA and stakeholder review
  • Launched beta, defined new functionality, then launched the full version

Results & Impact

A process-innovation tool that lets field users track captured information and get quantitative data in real time — significantly streamlining their process. Strict categorization and prioritization got v1 out in record time; a Lean approach delivered an MVP that could be iterated on, increasing acceptance and satisfaction.

  • Significantly streamlined field data-capture and tracking process
  • Strict categorization and prioritization got v1 out in record time
  • Lean MVP approach increased user acceptance and satisfaction through iteration

Business Goals

The platform gave field users a faster, more reliable way to document decision-mapping data, supporting better process innovation across Prosecutor's Offices and Attorneys General.
