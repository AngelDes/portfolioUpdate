Mapeo de Decisiones

A new client, a tight timeline, and an existing system sitting right there. We used it — rebranded, migrated, and shipped fast instead of starting over for the sake of starting over.

RoleProduct Designer
DurationNot specified
MethodologiesScrum, Design Thinking, Service Blueprints
ToolsSketch, Marvel, HTML/CSS, Protopie, VueJs, Miro

About

Mapeo de Decisiones captures and processes information to document decision-mapping by Public Prosecutor's Offices and Attorneys General across Mexico. It was a new engagement for our organization, under the same organization that had already delivered Polistat.

My Contribution

Product Designer on a project where the smartest first move was deciding what not to rebuild.

  • Defined project scope; documented data and process flows
  • Ran information architecture (card sorting), user stories, personas, and flows
  • Produced high and low-fidelity wireframes and interactive prototypes
  • Ran usability and heuristic testing; led QA and stakeholder review
  • Launched beta, defined new functionality, then launched the full version

Reuse, Not Rebuild

Polistat's component library already existed and already worked. Rather than build Mapeo's design system from zero — which would have cost time the timeline didn't have — I evolved Polistat's library into Mapeo's foundation: rebranded for the new client, and migrated the working files from Adobe XD to Sketch in the process. Less exciting than a from-scratch build. Considerably faster.

Results & Impact

Field users can now track captured information and get quantitative data in real time — a meaningful jump from whatever process existed before this. Strict categorization and prioritization got v1 out in record time; a Lean approach delivered an MVP built to be iterated on, not defended as final.

  • Significantly streamlined field data-capture and tracking process
  • Strict categorization and prioritization got v1 out in record time
  • Lean MVP approach increased user acceptance and satisfaction through iteration

Business Goals

The platform gave field users a faster, more reliable way to document decision-mapping data — and gave the organization proof that a shared system, reused deliberately, can serve a second client without a second client's worth of build time.
