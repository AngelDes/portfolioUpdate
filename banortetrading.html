Case Study · Fintech Web Application

Banorte Trading

A live trading platform. A redesign mid-development. A deadline that didn't move — so we didn't slow down.

Role
Lead UI Designer, Lead Product Designer
Duration
Not specified
Methodologies
Scrum, Design Thinking, Lean UX
Tools

About

Banorte Trading lets users execute trades, monitor markets, and buy and sell financial instruments. Real money, real markets, zero tolerance for a confusing interface.

My contribution

Lead UI Designer and Lead Product Designer. The job: take apart a platform already mid-build and put it back together better, without blowing the deadline.

  • Defined tasks and user personas; ran usability and A/B testing
  • Optimized flows and standardized workflows across the platform
  • Developed reusable components and implemented conventions and best practices
  • Modernized UI elements and ran accessibility evaluation
  • Built interactive prototypes and planned the platform restructuring

Redesigning mid-flight

The platform was already in development when the redesign started. No clean slate, no pause button. Deconstructing it into reusable components was the move that made a full redesign possible without stalling the build already underway.

Process

  1. 1

    First steps

    Once the need to update the application was defined, evaluations and interviews were conducted to define the direction the product would take.

  2. 2

    Idea validation

    The information architecture was improved and techniques such as wireframing and user personas were used.

  3. 3

    Mockups and prototype

    Prototypes and mockups were presented to validate the result and take it to the development team.

Results & impact

Full redesign, shipped on deadline, at an advanced stage of the development cycle — the hardest possible time to change course. Material Design guidance and rapid stakeholder iteration kept it realistic instead of ideal-but-late.

  • Shipped a complete redesign mid-development-cycle, on schedule
  • Met quality, usability, and human-centered design standards under a tight deadline
  • Material Design guidance and rapid stakeholder iteration kept scope realistic

Business goals

The redesign modernized the platform's UI and standardized its component library — positioning the product for faster, more consistent development going forward, not just a one-time fix.
