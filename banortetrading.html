About

Banorte Trading is an application for executing trades, monitoring financial markets, and buying and selling financial instruments.

My Contribution

As Lead UI Designer and Lead Product Designer, I led the deconstruction of the platform into reusable components and drove the complete redesign under a tight deadline.

  • Defined tasks and user personas; ran usability and A/B testing
  • Optimized flows and standardized workflows across the platform
  • Developed reusable components and implemented conventions/best practices
  • Modernized UI elements and ran accessibility evaluation
  • Built interactive prototypes and planned the platform restructuring

Process

First StepsEvaluations and interviews to define the product's direction.
Idea ValidationImproved IA; wireframing and user personas.
Mockups & PrototypePrototypes presented to validate results and hand off to development.

Results & Impact

By deconstructing the platform into reusable components, the team achieved a complete redesign at an advanced stage of the development cycle — using Material Design guidance and rapid stakeholder iteration to ship a new version on a tight deadline while meeting quality, usability, and human-centered design standards.

  • Product team successfully shipped a complete redesign mid-development-cycle
  • Met quality, usability, and human-centered design standards under a tight deadline
  • Material Design guidance and rapid stakeholder iteration kept scope realistic

Business Goals

The redesign modernized the trading platform's UI and standardized its component library, positioning the product for faster, more consistent future development.
