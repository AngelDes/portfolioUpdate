Polistat

A platform for capturing and georeferencing data to support police intelligence — designed end to end, from scope definition to full release.

RoleSr. Product Designer, Team Lead
Duration6 months
MethodologiesAtomic Design, Design Sprint, Design Thinking, Scrum
ToolsSketch, Adobe XD, Protopie, Laravel, VueJs

About

Polistat is a cloud-based application for capturing and georeferencing data used in police intelligence and analysis. The goal was straightforward to state and hard to deliver: improve decision-making, resource allocation, and intelligence operations through data that officers and analysts could actually use in the field, not just review after the fact.

My Contribution

I served as Lead Product Designer and UX/UI Designer for the full lifecycle of the project — from the first scoping conversations through delivery and the iterations that followed release.

  • Led understanding of the need and definition of scope; prepared the product roadmap
  • Defined business rules and development stages in collaboration with stakeholders from security institutions
  • Ran information architecture work (card sorting), user stories, personas, and flows
  • Produced wireframes and interactive prototypes; ran usability and heuristic testing
  • Led QA and stakeholder review cycles ahead of each release
  • Launched the beta, then the full version of the application

Working in a High-Stakes Domain

Designing for security institutions meant every decision had to be checked against operational and legal requirements before it could be considered a good decision at all. Business rules weren't a formality here — they came directly from how these institutions were legally and procedurally required to operate, and translating that into an interface meant staying close to stakeholders throughout, not just at kickoff.

Results & Impact

Qualitative

  • Resource management was reported as more efficient by users
  • Analysis of charts and reports was found to be more useful and accessible
  • Dynamic features such as heat maps and predictive intelligence were consistently cited as valuable

Roadmap Beyond v1

Future versions were scoped to include modules addressing gender and family violence cases — a direction that came directly out of user interviews during this engagement, not from a separate discovery phase. It's worth naming as part of the outcome: the platform's value extended past the initial scope because the research uncovered a need worth building for.

Business Goals

The solution met its primary business goals: improved resource allocation, streamlined administrative processes, and data-driven decision making for institutions where decisions carry real consequences. The platform gave the organization a foundation to build on rather than a finished, closed product.
