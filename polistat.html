Case Study · B2B Web Application

Polistat

A cloud-based platform for capturing and georeferencing data to improve decision-making and police intelligence — designed end-to-end from scope definition to full release.

RoleSr. Product Designer, Team Lead
Duration6 months
MethodologiesAtomic Design, Design Sprint, Design Thinking, Scrum
ToolsSketch, Adobe XD, Protopie, Laravel, VueJs

About

Polistat is a cloud-based web application that enables data capture and georeferencing for police intelligence and analysis, improving decision-making, resource allocation, and intelligence operations through data-driven insights.

My Contribution

I served as Lead Product Designer and UX/UI Designer throughout the entire process, from problem definition to delivery and implementation of the initial and subsequent iterations of the application.

  • Led understanding of the need and definition of scope; prepared the product roadmap
  • Defined business rules and development stages in collaboration with stakeholders from security institutions
  • Ran information architecture work (card sorting), user stories, personas, and flows
  • Produced wireframes and interactive prototypes; ran usability and heuristic testing
  • Led QA and stakeholder review cycles ahead of each release
  • Launched the beta, then the full version of the application

Leadership & Stakeholder Management

Working with security institutions required carefully translating operational and legal needs into product decisions. I owned the product from scope definition through roadmap and release, and planned the roadmap for future modules — including work on gender and family violence — based on needs surfaced during user interviews, positioning design as a driver of the long-term product vision rather than just the current release.

Process

Results & Impact

-60%Time to report an incident
-45%Time to generate reports
+30%Complete reports (first month)
+5%Data accuracy (first week)

Qualitative

  • Users reported more efficient resource management
  • Analysis of charts and reports was noted to be more useful and accessible
  • Dynamic features like heat maps and predictive intelligence were highly valued

Business Goals

The solution successfully met all primary business goals, improving resource allocation, streamlining processes, and enabling data-driven decision making. The platform created a foundation for continued improvements and additional modules, including future work on gender and family violence cases.
