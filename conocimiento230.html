About

Conocimiento 230 is a cloud-hosted digital platform for managing organizational resources — doubling as a repository of information and a records manager of the organization's collective experience.

My Contribution

As Lead Product Designer, I owned scope definition, roadmap and release planning, and led the team through research, prototyping, and validation using Scrum-based planning tools.

  • Led understanding of the need and definition of scope; prepared roadmap and release planning
  • Defined business rules and development stages; built work plan with Scrum tools
  • Produced wireframes, user stories, personas, and flows; ran UX research for data collection
  • Prototyped, gathered feedback, and iterated on validation of proposals
  • Ran usability and heuristic testing; led QA and stakeholder review
  • Launched the first version and planned next steps

Process

DefinitionScope and need defined with stakeholders to assess redesign feasibility.
IdeationSite map, IA, wireframes, and user stories developed.
PrototypeFunctional prototypes presented to evaluate ideas and hand off to development.
ValidationFirst version released and feedback collected.

Results & Impact

The redesign surfaced that earlier versions of the platform didn't actually solve the user's real need. Rebuilding around those research insights produced a tool that explores more scenarios and serves a wider range of user profiles.

  • Earlier versions were found not to solve the user's real underlying need
  • Rebuild around research insights let more scenarios and profiles be served

Business Goals

The redesign realigned the product with the organization's actual resource-management and knowledge-repository needs, giving the team a validated foundation to plan future releases from.
